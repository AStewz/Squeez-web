5 Features Your Digital Grocery List App Is Missing

By the Squeez team

Be honest: how do you keep track of your groceries? For most people it is a chaotic scramble. Maybe you use a crumpled piece of paper, a family group chat, or a long flat list in your phone's Notes app.

While those methods technically hold text, they do not actually help you shop. If you are still staring at a plain checklist and constantly scrolling up and down while trying to steer a grocery cart, you are doing it the hard way. Here are five game-changing features your current grocery list app is missing, and how Squeez uses them to completely change your supermarket routine.

1. Custom Category Mapping

With a standard checklist, ingredients appear in the exact order you thought of them. You might have apples at the top, milk in the middle and bananas at the very bottom. This leads to the ultimate grocery store frustration: walking all the way to the dairy aisle only to realise you have to walk right back to the produce section.

Squeez fixes this by letting you take total control. Instead of relying on rigid, algorithmic categorisation that gets it wrong, you can create your own custom categories right inside your recipes. When you walk into a section of the store, everything you need from that aisle is grouped perfectly together based on how you like to shop.

2. Fully Reorderable Sections to Match Your Local Store

Grouping your ingredients is incredibly helpful, but every supermarket layout is unique. Your local spot might put the bakery right at the entrance, while the store down the street starts with organic produce.

Squeez gives you the power to drag, drop and reorder your entire category list. You can arrange your custom sections to perfectly mimic the physical layout of your specific grocery store, allowing you to breeze through the aisles from entrance to checkout in one single, efficient loop.

3. Expanding and Collapsing Sections

When a grocery list gets long, it becomes overwhelming. Staring at a wall of 30 items makes it incredibly easy to skip past something important. With Squeez you can interact with your list dynamically:

  • Finished grabbing all your fruits and vegetables? Collapse the Produce category to instantly hide those items from view.
  • Need to focus strictly on what is left? Keep only the active categories expanded.

This keeps your phone screen clean, organised and stress-free while you navigate busy crowds.

4. Hide Checked Items

On a standard paper list, you cross an item off with a pen. On a basic phone app, you tap a circle to add a checkmark. In both cases, the item stays right there on the page, cluttering your view.

Squeez includes a Hide Checked Items toggle. The moment you check an item off, it instantly vanishes from your active view. Your list physically shrinks as you shop. Instead of scrolling past items already in your cart, you are left looking only at what still needs to be found.

5. Instant Multi-List Switching

Life is not a single grocery run. You should not have to mix your casual Monday night taco ingredients with the charcoal and paper plates you need for a Saturday barbecue.

Squeez lets you create entirely separate, independent lists and flip between them with a single tap. Keep your Weekly Meal Prep entirely separate from your Costco Bulk Run or your Target Errands, keeping your headspace completely organised.

If your current grocery list is just a flat wall of text, it is costing you time and energy. Upgrade to a shopping assistant that does the heavy lifting for you.
