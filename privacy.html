Privacy Policy

Squeez - Recipe Management App · Last updated: April 2026
This policy explains what personal data Squeez collects, how it is used, and your rights under UK data protection law.

1. Who We Are

Squeez is operated by Alexander John Stewart, an individual based in the United Kingdom.

Contact: hello@squeez.uk

Squeez is intended for users aged 13 and over. By using the app you confirm you meet this requirement.

This policy is governed by UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Anonymous Sessions

When you open Squeez for the first time, an anonymous session is created automatically - no sign-up required. At this point, only two pieces of data are written to our database:

  • A randomly generated user ID
  • A flag indicating the session is anonymous (no name, email, or any personal information is stored)

Anonymous sessions that are never converted to a full account are automatically deleted after 60 days.

3. Creating an Account

You can optionally create a free account using your email address and a password. When you do, your anonymous session is upgraded - your user ID is preserved and your email address is added to your account record.

You can delete your account at any time from within the app. All data - including your recipes, recipe photos, usage history, and account information - is deleted immediately and permanently. There is no grace period or archive.

4. Recipe Imports and AI Processing

Squeez supports several ways to import recipes. Here is exactly what happens with your data in each case. If you create a recipe manually by typing it in, the content you enter is stored in your account like any other recipe - no AI processing is involved.

URL and web imports

The page content at the URL you submit is sent to OpenAI's API (using the GPT-4o-mini model) to extract structured recipe data. No personal information is included in this request - only the page content itself.

Share extension (Instagram, TikTok, and other apps)

When you share content from another app using the iOS share extension, only the text caption of the shared content is extracted and processed. No video, image, or binary media is downloaded or stored at any point.

Photo imports

When you import a recipe from a photo, text recognition is performed entirely on your device using ML Kit (a Google SDK). The image itself is never transmitted to our servers or to OpenAI. Only the extracted text is sent for recipe parsing - identical to a text import. The photo is not stored anywhere by Squeez.

Async processing

For imports that are processed in the background, the raw submitted content (URL page text or caption) is temporarily stored while the import is in progress. It is permanently deleted from our systems immediately upon successful import. Only the structured recipe output is retained.

OpenAI logging

OpenAI prompts and responses are not logged or stored in our database.

5. Recipe Photos

You can optionally attach a cover photo to any recipe, chosen from your photo library or camera. These photos are uploaded to and stored on our backend (Supabase) so they can be displayed in the app.

Recipe photos are entirely optional - Squeez is fully functional without them.

If you remove a recipe photo, both the image file and the reference to it are permanently deleted from our systems immediately. Upon account deletion, all recipe photos are deleted along with all other user data.

6. What Data We Collect and Store

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect and store the following:

  • Randomly generated user ID - collected for all users, including anonymous sessions
  • Email address - collected only when you create a free or premium account
  • Recipes and recipe content - whether created manually or imported
  • Optional recipe cover photos
  • Source URL of imported recipes
  • Shopping lists and their contents
  • App preferences and organisation - including folder names, shopping list names, ingredient category labels, and ordering preferences
  • Usage data - such as the number of AI imports you have used
  • Behavioural analytics events stored in our own database (for example: onboarding steps completed, import source used, paywall views, account deletion). These are linked to your user ID but not to your name or email address.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Squeez. Where relevant, we describe what data each receives.

Supabase

Supabase provides our database, authentication system, and backend infrastructure including photo storage. Your user data is stored on Supabase's servers. See Supabase's privacy policy for details.

OpenAI

Recipe text content is sent to OpenAI for AI processing. No personally identifiable information or image data is included in these requests. See OpenAI's privacy policy for details.

ML Kit (Google)

ML Kit is used for on-device text recognition when you import a recipe from a photo. All image processing happens entirely on your device - no data is sent to Google by Squeez as part of this process.

Sentry

Sentry is used for crash reporting and error monitoring. Sentry's SDK automatically captures information such as device type, OS version, and error context. We do not set any explicit user identifiers in Sentry. Please refer to Sentry's privacy policy for details of what their SDK captures by default.

RevenueCat

RevenueCat manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. When you subscribe, your Supabase user ID is shared with RevenueCat to manage your entitlement. No additional personal data is passed to RevenueCat.

Apple

All payments are processed by Apple via the App Store. Squeez does not collect or store any payment card information. Apple's privacy policy governs payment data.

8. Subscriptions and Payments

Squeez offers a free tier and a premium subscription. All payments are handled by Apple through the App Store - we never see or store your payment card details. Subscription management is handled via RevenueCat.

9. Data Retention

  • Anonymous sessions: deleted after 60 days of inactivity
  • Full accounts: retained until you delete your account
  • Upon account deletion: all data including recipes, shopping lists, photos, and organisational data is deleted immediately and permanently
  • Raw import content: deleted immediately upon successful import
  • Recipe photos: deleted immediately when removed by you

10. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident, you have the following rights regarding your personal data.

Right of access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification

You can ask us to correct any inaccurate personal data we hold.

Right to erasure

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. For Squeez, the simplest and most immediate way to exercise this right is to delete your account from within the app - this deletes all your data immediately and permanently. You can also contact us directly at hello@squeez.uk.

Right to restriction of processing

In certain circumstances, you can ask us to restrict how we process your data.

Right to data portability

You can request your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

Right to object

You have the right to object to processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@squeez.uk. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data is being handled unlawfully.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review the policy periodically.
