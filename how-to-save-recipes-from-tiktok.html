How to Save Recipes from TikTok to Your Phone (The Fast Way)

By the Squeez team

Ever found the perfect 60-second dinner idea on TikTok, saved it to your favourites, and then completely forgot it existed? Or worse, you went back to cook it only to spend 15 minutes scrubbing through the video trying to find the actual ingredient measurements?

TikTok is incredible for food inspiration, but it is a terrible digital cookbook. If you want to know how to save recipes from TikTok to your phone as clean, organised text instead of messy video bookmarks, there is a much easier way. Here is how to turn any cooking video into a structured recipe in seconds.

The Problem with Saving Recipes on TikTok

When you hit the Save icon on TikTok, you are only saving a link to a video. You are not actually saving a recipe. This leaves you with a few major headaches:

  • No quick ingredient lists: You have to pause and rewind the video constantly just to write down a shopping list.
  • Missing details: Creators often leave out exact measurements or step-by-step instructions because caption space is limited.
  • Lost content: If a creator deletes their video or changes their account, your saved dinner plan disappears forever.

Fortunately, you can automatically extract the ingredients and method from any video using a dedicated recipe saver app like Squeez.

How to Import and Save TikTok Recipes in Seconds

Instead of taking screenshots or typing out notes manually, you can use your phone's built-in share extension to send any cooking video straight to Squeez. The app automatically pulls the details, breaks down the steps, and builds a clean recipe card for you.

Here is exactly how it works:

Step 1 - Tap the Share button

When you find a recipe video on TikTok, tap the Share button (the arrow icon) on the right side of your screen.

Step 2 - Select Squeez from your menu

Scroll across the app options and tap More, then select Squeez. Pro tip: you can edit your phone's share sheet to add Squeez to your favourites so you can skip the More step next time.

Step 3 - Wait for the magic

The import engine kicks in and redirects you to the Squeez app. It takes just 7 to 15 seconds to analyse the video and extract the text.

Step 4 - Review and save

Squeez presents you with a clean method and ingredients breakdown. Give it a quick glance, customise anything you like, and hit Save to add it to your personal digital cookbook.

Revisit the Original Video Anytime

One of the best parts about using Squeez is that you never lose the source material. While the app cleanly strips out the noise and breaks down the method into text steps, it automatically saves the original TikTok link alongside the recipe. If you ever want to check the creator's specific technique or watch the video again, just tap the link inside the recipe card to jump right back.

Beyond TikTok: Save Recipes from Anywhere

The same quick share-extension trick does not just stop at TikTok. Squeez handles recipe imports from across the web:

  • Instagram Reels: Use the exact same share extension workflow to pull recipes straight from your favourite food creators.
  • Food blogs and websites: Share any URL from Safari or Chrome directly to the app to bypass the life stories and ads, getting straight to the recipe card.
  • Physical cookbooks and handwritten notes: Take a quick photo inside the app and Squeez will digitise it into editable text using on-device text recognition.

Stop scrolling through hundreds of saved videos trying to figure out what to make for dinner. Download Squeez and convert your favourite social media recipes into organised, searchable, cookable cards.
