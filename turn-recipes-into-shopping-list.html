How to Turn Online Recipes into a Shopping List Instantly

By the Squeez team

We have all been there: you find three incredible recipes on TikTok or a food blog and decide to cook them this week. But then comes the tedious part. You open your Notes app, look at the first recipe, type out 2 cloves of garlic. Then you switch to the second recipe, see it needs 3 cloves of garlic, delete what you wrote, and type 5 cloves of garlic.

By the time you have finished compiling ingredients for the week, you have spent 15 minutes switching between tabs and doing unnecessary kitchen maths. If you want to know how to turn online recipes into a shopping list instantly, there is a much better way.

The Problem with Manual Grocery Lists

Writing out your grocery list by hand or in a basic text app is a recipe for supermarket frustration:

  • The back-and-forth scramble: Toggling between web pages and your checklist while steering a grocery cart is stressful.
  • Over-buying ingredients: If you do not manually combine quantities across different meals, you end up buying three bags of onions when you only needed four individual ones.
  • Forgetting essentials: It is easy to skip over a minor ingredient like a tablespoon of cornstarch when you are rushing to copy things down.

How to Auto-Generate Your Grocery List in Seconds

Instead of doing the heavy lifting yourself, Squeez uses a smart import engine that automatically reads your saved recipes and compiles your shopping list for you.

Step 1 - Multi-select your meals

Open the Squeez app, navigate to the shopping list, tap Recipes and browse your saved digital cookbook. Tap the recipes you plan to cook this week and hit Done. Squeez instantly merges ingredients into a unified list sorted by category.

Step 2 - Choose individual ingredients

Do not want to add items you already have? Squeez lets you select or deselect individual ingredients from within a recipe before building the list. If you already have olive oil and salt in the pantry, just skip them.

A Smart List Built for the Grocery Store

Once Squeez generates your grocery list, it becomes an interactive shopping assistant rather than a flat piece of text. Here is what you can do while walking the aisles:

  • Aisle-by-aisle categorisation: Squeez automatically groups ingredients into logical categories like Produce, Dairy and Pantry. No more running back across the store because you missed an item.
  • Custom layouts: Every grocery store is different. Squeez lets you drag and reorder categories to match the exact layout of your local supermarket.
  • Clean screen view: As you grab items, check them off. You can collapse completed categories or hide checked items entirely so your list physically shrinks as you shop.

Manage Multiple Lists at Once

Planning a Tuesday night dinner requires a very different mindset to shopping for a weekend barbecue. Squeez lets you create separate, independent lists and switch between them instantly. Keep your Weekly Meal Prep list entirely separate from your Dinner Party or Costco Run lists.

Your phone is a supercomputer. There is no reason you should still be manually copying ingredients from a website into a digital notepad. Download Squeez and automate your next grocery run.
